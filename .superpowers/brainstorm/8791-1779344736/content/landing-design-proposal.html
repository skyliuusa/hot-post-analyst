Page structure

  • Sticky compact nav with one primary CTA.
  • Asymmetric hero: left conversion copy, right product screenshot area.
  • Trust strip directly under CTA with concrete operational proof.
  • Workflow section: Discover, Teardown, Draft, Publish, Review.
  • Screenshot-led feature bento with live signals, hook extraction, weekly briefs.
  • Pricing and final CTA designed as direct decision points, not generic marketing blocks.

Visual system

  • Neutral zinc/slate base, off-white surfaces, one restrained teal accent.
  • Geist or Satoshi-style sans typography; no serif, no Inter dependency assumption.
  • CSS-only motion: staggered reveal, shimmer skeletons, subtle active press states.
  • No emoji, no purple-blue AI gradient, no generic three-card feature row.
  • Mobile collapses to single column with screenshot after the primary CTA.
